## What helps ranking beyond keywords
1. Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster: add the site, submit `/sitemap.xml`.
2. Google Business Profile (name, phone, service area Bangladesh), and keep the same name/phone everywhere.
3. Facebook/YouTube posts that link to specific pages (for example a short video: "নামজারি করার নিয়ম" with the page link).
4. Write one useful article or FAQ answer per month with the phrases above (district/upazila names help local search).
5. Keep the site fast and mobile friendly (already done), and never copy text from other sites.

Note: nobody can guarantee a Google position. These steps give the site the technical and content basics.
